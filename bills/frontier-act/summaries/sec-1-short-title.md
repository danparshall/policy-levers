# SEC. 1. SHORT TITLE — summary

**One-line:** Names the Act the "FRONTIER Act" — Frontier Risk Oversight, National Transparency, Independent Evaluation, and Reporting Act.

## What it does

Assigns the Act's short title. No operative content, no defined terms, no cross-references. The acronym is worth unpacking for readers who won't have it memorized: **F**rontier **R**isk **O**versight, **N**ational **T**ransparency, **I**ndependent **E**valuation, and **R**eporting. The four framing verbs (oversight, transparency, evaluation, reporting) map roughly onto the bill's operative sections — Sec. 4 (transparency/reporting), Sec. 5 (independent verification), Sec. 8 (emergency oversight).

## Changes vs GAAIA discussion draft (2026-06-04)

Two changes from the analogue in GAAIA § 1:

- **New name.** "Great American Artificial Intelligence Act of 2026" becomes the "FRONTIER Act." The rebrand tracks the bill's narrower scope — FRONTIER is only the frontier-oversight slice of GAAIA (Title I §§ 101, 102, 111, 112, 113, 121), stripped of workforce, cybersecurity, R&D, fraud, and free-speech titles.
- **No table of contents.** GAAIA § 1(b) carried a full 46-section TOC spanning four titles. FRONTIER § 1 is short-title-only — a compact 9-section bill doesn't need a formal TOC.
