## Strategic assessment

**Why this matters now.** The threat isn't that Congress ignores AI; it's that Congress legislates on AI too late or too badly. The last year produced real motion (the GAAIA discussion draft, H.R. 9363, S.2938), but the technical capacity gap in member offices means bills arrive with bugs that gut them (a fine cap that punishes AI fraud LESS than ordinary fraud; a citation to an executive order rescinded eighteen months earlier) and structural gaps that make them visibility regimes rather than control regimes. If nobody acts, the first binding federal AI framework gets written by whoever shows up, and the people showing up are overwhelmingly industry lobbyists. The counterfactual isn't "no regulation"; it's regulation with no teeth and no measurement wired into any decision.

**Why this intervention.** I'm strengthening the constrain layer primarily: getting oversight machinery (transparency requirements, audit regimes, whistleblower protections) through Congress in bindable form. The economic-impact work also feeds withstand, giving policymakers labor-disruption analysis before mass displacement rather than after. The specific gap I'm filling: retail-scale congressional education with economics as the coalition-neutral entry point. CSET and FAS write excellent reports; Horizon places excellent fellows; nobody is walking fifty offices with original labor-impact analysis they authored themselves and a standing offer of technical briefings. Economic framing reaches offices that are allergic to safety framing, and it holds the full range of concerns (existential risk, job loss, human dignity) under one tent. My comparative advantage is the combination: I translate nerd-to-normie well (10,000+ hours of speaking experience, including pitching radiation-detection technology to Latin American governments), I have my own economic framework rather than a summary of someone else's, and as a one-person shop I can turn around a 269-page bill analysis while larger organizations are still scheduling their review panels.
