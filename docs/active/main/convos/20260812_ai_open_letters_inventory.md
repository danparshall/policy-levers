## Provisional Findings

- **30+ distinct letters** clustering into 9 categories. Two orgs dominate x-risk (FLI + CAIS); creative/copyright cluster is numerically the largest single-issue mobilization (Newton-Rex 50,000+).
- **Signatory counts drift by source and date** and every count must be cited with its observation date (Pause: 27,565 May 2023 → 30,000+ March 2024; Superintelligence: 700+ launch → 32,214 late Oct 2025; Newton-Rex: 11,500 → 19,000 → 50,000+).
- **Six flagship letters** for policymaker briefings by name-recognition + coalition breadth: Pause (2023), CAIS Statement (2023), Right to Warn (2024), Global Call for AI Red Lines (2025), Statement on Superintelligence (2025), We Must Act Now (2026).
- **Six "super-signers"** form the connective tissue of the x-risk/governance coalition (Bengio, Hinton, Russell, Tegmark, Harari, Wozniak). Cross-letter contradictions are analytically valuable (Musk/xAI is the sharpest).
- **Gap topics with no flagship letter**: healthcare/clinical AI, journalism (lawsuits but no unified letter), education, environment/datacenter energy, financial-systems/systemic risk, Chinese-language domestic letters.
- **Red Lines assessment (Transformer News, Sept 2025):** "unlikely to move the needle on concrete governance" near-term due to US opposition (Cruz + Trump AI Action Plan). Worth caveating in Hill-facing use.
