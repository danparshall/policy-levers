# Memo: H.R. 9363 vs GAAIA CAISI — floor or ceiling

- Date: 2026-07-17 (evening); task issue #2
- Source: BILLS-119hr9363ih (govinfo; saved at bills/hr9363/). CAVEAT: introduced text; reported (rh) version post-6/25 markup not yet on GPO — reconcile when posted (NOTES records an Obernolte removal amendment offered/withdrawn at markup).

## Verdict: ceiling as drafted, with a study-shaped escape hatch

Not two funding levels for one agency; two incompatible agencies drafted into the SAME statutory slot.

1. **Section collision.** Both bills add a new § 5304 to title LIII of the National AI Initiative Act of 2020. GAAIA § 102: "Center for AI Standards and Innovation." 9363 § 2: "Center for AI Security and Innovation." GAAIA's Title I cross-references its § 5304 throughout (e.g., IVO discipline via § 5304(c)(1)(X)(vii)). If 9363 enacts first, GAAIA § 102 is impossible as drafted; every dependent cross-reference breaks; the path becomes repeal-and-replace of a just-enacted section.
2. **Express anti-regulatory locks in 9363:** (i) Director gets no regulatory, rulemaking, or enforcement authority; (l)(1)-(3) rules of construction: no new enforcement authority, no regulatory authority conferred on ANY government, no modification of any reporting requirement; (e)(2)(B) statutory use-immunity — information shared with the Director may not be used by any Federal/State/local/Tribal government to regulate the sharing entity. Inverse of GAAIA's mandatory-report-and-act CAISI. Post-9363, GAAIA must expressly strip an immunity labs will have relied on.
3. **Voluntary-only architecture:** evaluations via voluntary agreements ((c)(1)(B), incl. classified CBRN/cyber evals); standards development/adoption voluntary ((c)(1)(D),(F)). "Covered frontier system" defined by the Director ((c)(4)), vs GAAIA's statutory 10^26 + revenue thresholds — two definitional regimes for the same concept in the same title.
4. **Escape hatch:** (a)(3) transfer study — Secretary MAY study making the Center a Commerce agency, with recommendations to Congress on additional authority and funding, 30-day committee review. Growth contemplated, but only via a future act of Congress.
5. **Structure facts:** $20M/yr FY2027-2032; 5-year sunset on the section; hiring cap = 15-person ceiling on special direct-hire authority ((d)(2)), ordinary Title 5 hiring unaffected ((d)(1)) — correct our "hiring cap" shorthand accordingly. FOIA exemption for shared info ((e)(1)). International engagement except with foreign adversaries ((g)).
6. **9363's own bug:** body names the center "Security and Innovation"; its clerical TOC amendment inserts "Sec. 5304. Center for AI Standards and Innovation." Internal inconsistency, evidently inherited from GAAIA/FAIIA text. Technical-correction material for Science-lane outreach; also evidence of shared drafting DNA.
