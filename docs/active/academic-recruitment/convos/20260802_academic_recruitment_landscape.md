5. Dan called the pitch: frame academic recruitment as a Manhattan Project / Vannevar Bush activity for AI Policy folks. Claude pulled the Einstein-Szilard letter (links in RESEARCH_LOG; session ran on the letter's 87th anniversary, Aug 2) and distilled the Bush playbook (six moves, see RESEARCH_LOG).

Core pitch insight: the Einstein letter's own recommendation #2 was "fund the academics in place," and it produced only the $6,000 Briggs committee — the myth credits the letter, the mechanism was OSRD. AI analog: CAIS statement (2023) = the Einstein letter; the Bush layer is missing.
