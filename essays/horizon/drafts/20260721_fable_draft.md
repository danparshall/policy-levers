**Recommendation:** The State Department's Bureau of Arms Control, Nonproliferation, and Stability should codify the November 2024 Biden–Xi affirmation of human control over the decision to use nuclear weapons — currently declaratory, undefined, and unverified — into a negotiated U.S.–China framework on AI in nuclear command, control, and communications (NC3), then extend it to the P5. First steps: table the issue in the strategic-stability channel; agree definitions (launch authority vs. decision support vs. early warning); commission joint verification-concepts work.

**The problem is commitment logic.** Schelling showed that surrendering control makes threats credible; Kavka noted that rational agents cannot sincerely intend retaliation that saves nothing and kills millions. The Cold War bridged the paradox with training and duty — but Arkhipov reconsidered, and from the posture designer's chair he was a reliability failure. Moscow's answer was Perimeter. AI is the natural extension: it resolves Kavka's paradox because the machine doesn't need to *want* retaliation; it only needs to lack the ability to change its mind.

The alignment literature calls that ability corrigibility. Every alignment desideratum — interruptibility, human-in-the-loop, a reliable off-switch — is anti-commitment. Posture designers chasing credibility will spec systems that safety engineers are trying to make impossible. To at least one important customer, a corrigible AI is a defective product. That is not a failure mode markets or doctrine will self-correct; it is what the incentives, uncorrected, produce.

**The spiral is already loading.** AI accelerates the offensive side of the NC3 fear cycle. It doesn't have to pwn an adversary's NC3; Beijing only has to worry that it might have, and cannot prove otherwise — absence-of-intrusion is exactly what you cannot verify. Entanglement makes it worse: NC3 shares sensors and networks with conventional C2, so espionage is indistinguishable from disarming-strike prep (Acton 2018). A commander who believes the channel may be compromised faces pressure to pre-delegate — to machines whose selling point is that they cannot be recalled.

**Why State, why this seam.** The Biden–Xi statement is a live diplomatic hook that costs nothing to build on and is worth nothing until someone does: no definitions, no scope, no verification path. Force-modernization decisions on both sides will foreclose the option space within a few years.

NC3-AI integration is where arms control has to move next, and nobody is negotiating it. The Department can be first.
