## The one-line correction to our prior model of him

He is NOT a MAD-survives sanguinist. He is closer to the anti-complacency pole than we
are, and got there by Python simulation in 2021. Our essay's rhetorical foil
(Winter-Levy & Lalwani's *Foreign Affairs* complacency) is not Chang's view. So a
"response to Chang" that tries to convince him AI is destabilizing misreads the room by
four years.

## What he argues (nuclear essay)

- Method: quasi-geographic Python sim, each Chinese road-mobile TEL as a state machine
  over 24h, low/medium/high alert. Models detection + identification + tracking; four
  metrics (undetected roaming, effective counterforce area, surviving TELs, second-strike
  probability).
- Headline: deep-learning intelligence-processing speeds target-location in
  already-held intelligence by 1–3 orders of magnitude; cuts "effective counterforce
  area" by 1–2 orders. He frames the effect size as equivalent to inflating the US
  arsenal by 1,000–100,000%.
- BUT alert-contingent, and he's careful about it:
  - Low alert: China lacks secure second strike *even without AI*, largely because TELs
    are de-mated and tied to bases they return to — a non-AI reason. (So low-alert is not
    really an AI story; he says so.)
  - Medium alert: without AI humans can't keep pace (retaliation prob → 1, ~20–30 mated
    TELs survive); with AI the US gets brief, stochastic first-strike windows that require
    a visibly ready posture China would detect and react to.
  - **High alert: counterforce unworkable with OR without AI, due to irreducible latency**
    (minimum collect+analyze time + minimum weapon flight time). His own physics floor,
    on the *timing* axis.
- Theoretical frame: nests the result in unipolarity theory (Monteiro; Lieber-Press
  *Myth of the Nuclear Revolution*). If AI structurally makes counterforce thinkable,
  even a benevolent hegemon can't credibly signal restraint → explains intensifying
  US-China competition. Bigger than "is MAD stable"; it's a balance-of-power argument.
- Exchange-rate core: "US arsenal large, China's small; everything else just adjusts the
  exchange rate." This is the load-bearing intuition that makes the result robust to
  parameter uncertainty.

## Things he already did that our essay currently treats as fresh

- **Scud-hunt-is-disanalogous** (his numbers: ~2,500 sorties, 0 confirmed kills vs 30
  Iraqi TELs, 88 missiles launched). Three-part disanalogy to the China case *before* AI:
  (1) DF-31A signature far larger than a Scud (~10x heavier, ~4x larger) + unique
  fire-control/power/aiming/inspection support trucks AI can learn; (2) nuclear barrage
  tolerates targeting error conventional Scud strikes couldn't; (3) US would do the
  "intelligence preparation of the battlefield" the 1991 coalition skipped.
  → If our essay leans on the Scud hunt as evidence TELs are hard to find, he has a
  published rebuttal to exactly that. Reframe our use accordingly.
- **Proliferated-constellation resilience** (satellite miniaturization → super-linear
  small-sat constellation growth). This is our Starlink/NRO argument in 2021 form.
- **Counter-space / ASAT entanglement** and the **cyber training-time-attack menu**
  (data poisoning, adversarial examples), overlapping our destabilizing-response taxonomy.
- **Cascade to other dyads** (India–Pakistan, NK, Iran; SK conventional counterforce)
  and the **nonproliferation-deterrent inversion** (AI counterforce may deter new
  entrants — crossing the single-weapon threshold no longer buys safety).

## The two genuine gaps (where our independent route went and his bracketed)

1. **Undersea SNR floor.** He EXPLICITLY brackets the undersea balance as "future work"
   and rests on the *assumption* it favors the US. He never engages the physics of whether
   a quiet, deep, slow boat is findable in principle. Our Bernoulli-hump cubic-scaling /
   SNR-floor treatment is a real contribution here, not a restatement. This is the
   strongest extension — it's least dependent on Chang being the reader (it'd be additive
   for any reader).
2. **Custody vs. detection as distinct bins.** His model improves detection/ID/tracking
   wholesale by orders of magnitude. Our taxonomy separates processing-limited *detection*
   (AI helps enormously — we agree) from revisit-geometry-limited *custody* (bought with
   launch cadence, not algorithms). Underdeveloped in him rather than absent → this is a
   "sharpen," not a "he missed it." Verify against his TEL-behavior/tracking mechanics
   before asserting it as a clean gap.
