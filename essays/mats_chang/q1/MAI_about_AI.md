"a Mutual Assured Destruction [posture] as a goal is, almost literally, mad. MAD." - Donald Brennan

Thinking about MAD is like a trip through the looking-glass: defense is unstable, offense is pointless, and insanity is rational.  That doesn't make it WRONG, but it's disconcerting nevertheless.

In a Foreign Affairs article from July 2025, [AUTHORS] argue that AI ultimately won't be dramatically destabilizing to the decades-long doctorine of MAD.  They claim that three core pieces of MAD remain in place, to wit:
    - second-strike capabilities remain functional
    - command-and-control networks can't be completely removed
    - missile defense faces an asymetry that favors the attacker

I am far less sanguine, for the following reasons:
    - sensor linking to produce coherent trails of TEL locations and subs.  I have done this, both in Physics and in Aviation (not easy, but possible)
    - Mythos could pwn them... it DEFINITELY makes them scared of being pwned
    - more speculative, but developments in AI could enable better discrimination between missles with warheads and those with decoys

In any event, I fully agree with them that the mere PROSPECT of AI could be change default postures.  Improved algorithms (nearly certain with AGI) and sensor technology (certainly possible) could enable LOW as a position of even smaller nuclear states.
