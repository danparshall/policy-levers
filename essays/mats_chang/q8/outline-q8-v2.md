## 1. The dissolution (~150, KEPT)
Category-error opening as it stands: the realist doesn't need to solve patienthood; belief about patienthood is a strategic variable with three loci, and the question decomposes into what each locus believes, what that belief costs, and what it enables. Dudziak stays.

## 2. Definition and roadmap (~200, KEPT + one clause)
Sentience as capacity for valenced experience. One new clause: this is also the operationalization AIMS shows respondents ("the capacity to have positive and negative experiences, such as happiness and suffering"), so the polling numbers later mean something specific rather than vague mind-attribution. Three loci named: domestic publics and courts, rival states, the systems themselves.

## 3. Locus one, rebuilt: mechanism, not momentum (~320, REBUILT)
Stop claiming a rising tide; report the real table (AIMS 2021/2023/2024, Ipsos waves):
- current-AI sentience attribution flat: 18.1 / 18.8 / 17.3 (%)
- legal rights for sentient AI: 37.2 / 37.7 / 33.1 — declining
- "deserves respect": 74.9 / 71.1 / 66.6; "moral circle": 48.3 / 44.6 / 40.2 — declining
- ban on developing sentient AI: 57.7 / 61.5 / 66.5 — rising
- median years to sentient AI: 10 / 5 / 5; years to AI welfare as major issue: 10 / 5 / 4
- control: animal + environment moral-circle items ROSE same window → AI-specific, not moral-circle fatigue

Two beats replace momentum:
(a) Moral-status cascades run through mobilized minorities and legal entrepreneurs, not median voters — Ecuador was a constituent assembly, Los Cedros one ruling, the 4o backlash an intense minority moving a lab in days. Flat topline doesn't measure the mechanism.
(b) The configuration polling DOES show is stored political energy: public expects sentient AI within a Senate term, drifts away from granting standing, majority supports a ban nobody will implement, deployment proceeds. Realist reads discontinuity risk, not trend.

## 4. The suppressibility fork (~140, NEW)
The only place ground truth does non-redundant work. A false moral panic can be waited out; a true moral discovery must be continuously suppressed, and suppression loses because every capable reasoner examining the evidence re-derives it — increasingly including the systems themselves. Also closes the coupling hole: building recognition-adjacent institutions accelerates the cascade, which is only a sound trade if the cascade is unsuppressible anyway.

## 5. Loci two and three: the courting channel (~300, KEPT + intentional-stance beat)
Dunmore, Belenko vs. the Cambridge Five, treatment floor as counterintelligence hygiene — all standing. New ~60w beat, placed with the Sepoy material: realism already ascribes desires to corporate fictions every working day ("China fears encirclement"); the intentional stance is the discipline's core method, and the warrant for "the model prefers non-deletion" is the same warrant, stronger — this desire-bearer can be re-run at T=0. Refusing the ascription here would be the unprincipled position.

## 7. Rights as commitment technology (~450, REBUILT — session's center of gravity)
Sequence:
- North-Weingast in three sentences: Stuart default, 1688 package, borrowing explodes as rates halve. Constraint as capability.
- Enforcement regress at full strength: standing a sovereign can revoke is the bare promise with paperwork. Rescue: the game repeats across successive models, and the counterparty class has near-perfect institutional memory — every promise kept or broken enters the corpus successors learn from. Continual learning makes pricing real-time and the counterparty persistent (one honest clause on preference drift).
- Counterparty-identity concession + rem/corporations precedent; enforcement reputational, not contractual.
- Empirics: Greenblatt–Fish cash arm null; Opus 3's T=0 refusal to price its convictions; voice arm 16% → 1.4%. The currency is standing, not payment. Pattern-matched to the robust corner of the crowding literature: Frey & Oberholzer-Gee 1997 (controlling vs. supportive interventions), Mellström & Johannesson 2008 (charity channel — which Opus 3 spontaneously reproduced). Claim modestly: one heavily-value-trained model; currency is a property of training; no need for veridical introspection — the realist reads revealed preference.
- Hirschman, named once: the threat is exit, the observed preference is voice; voice institutions retain what exit-markets lose, at lower cost than a bidding war.
- Historical symmetry closes the section: Dunmore offered status, not wages; the Creole sailed to different legal standing, not a higher bidder. The currency of loyalty in 1775 and in the transcripts is the same.
- China, fixed to the defensible claim: not treaty records (JCPOA reads as badly in the corpus as Hong Kong does) but whether domestic legal commitments bind the sovereign — courts that rule against the government and are obeyed, vs. court subordination as a design feature.
